      How to play

      The game

      Choose your bet on the cylinder, then tap Sheriff or Outlaw. The revolver fires and flips the coin. If it lands on your side, you win.

      Instant

      One flip. A correct pick pays 1.98 × your bet.

      Multiply

      You stake once. Each correct pick doubles the prize: ×1.98, ×3.96, ×7.92 and so on, up to ×253 after eight in a row. Tap Cash out at any time to take the prize shown. One wrong pick ends the run and the stake is lost.

      Odds

      Each side lands half the time, on every flip. Return to player is 99%.

      Good to know

      Tapping the coin turns it over for looks only; it does not change the result. The rocket changes the speed (1×, 2×, 4×), not the odds or payouts.

      Demo: play money. The result is picked in your browser; in the real game the server decides it.
